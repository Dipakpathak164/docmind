# AetherCorp Employee Handbook & Company Policies

Welcome to AetherCorp! This document outlines key policies, benefits, and guidelines for all employees. Please read through these guidelines to ensure a productive and safe work environment.

## 1. Work Locations and Remote Policy
AetherCorp operates under a hybrid work model. 
- All employees are eligible to work remotely for up to three (3) days per week.
- The remaining two (2) days must be spent in our primary office location.
- Core collaboration hours are between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST. During these hours, all team members are expected to be online and available for meetings.

## 2. Annual Leave and Time Off
We believe in a healthy work-life balance.
- Standard full-time employees receive twenty-five (25) days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.
- We also offer five (5) personal wellness days, which can be taken with short notice for mental health or minor illnesses.

## 3. Office Guidelines & Pet Policy
We are proud to run a dog-friendly office under specific conditions:
- Dogs are welcome in the office on "Pawsome Thursdays" only.
- Owners must ensure their dogs are leashed, quiet, and friendly to others.
- A maximum of five (5) dogs are allowed in the office simultaneously. Registration must be done on the company intranet beforehand.

## 4. Kitchen Amenities and Facilities
- The office kitchen is fully stocked with snacks and drinks.
- The premium espresso coffee machine is located on the 3rd floor.
- Please note that the espresso machine requires a blue security fob to operate. If you do not have a fob, please contact office operations on the 1st floor.
